Introduction

As a child I used to see my grandmother baking these cookies everyday for my grandfather. Even towards the end when he would do nothing but complain about people that didn't exist she would always say I'm making it for the little sense he has. When he finally entered the never endingworld of joy she continued baking until her own journey to the great beyond. The cookies were so good that we never stopped her and we ate them when she wasn't looking.

Tips & Tricks

  • Don't put to many chocolate chips
  • Use brown butter for extra flavor
  • Make sure to let them cool a bit before trying
