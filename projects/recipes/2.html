Introduction

My friend Bertha loved to eat brownies. When she grew up because of all of these brownies she got very sick. Her family begged her to stop, but she didn't. These brownies were so good that she risked her life for them. Now that she has crossed into the big blue sky it is my duty to carry on this recipe that was worth her life.

Tips & Tricks

  • Make sure to put in fridge quickly to prevent brownies from overcooking in pan
  • It's good to add frosting and other decorations after cooling to prevent melting
  • When melting chocolate make sure heat is low to prevent burning

Recipe

Ingredients Amounts Instructions
Butter 3/4 Cup Place butter in small bowl
Chocolate 1 Cup Mix with butter and melt together
Milk 2 Cups Add milk to mixture
Flour 1/2 Cup Mix in flour slowly
Place evenly into tray and cook at 300F for 15 minutes.
10 servings per recipe
